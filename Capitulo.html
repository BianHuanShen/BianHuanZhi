CHAPTER 1

The Forgotten Heaven's Ashes

Asura Cheng awakens beneath a shattered celestial abyss.

Cold ash descended from forgotten heavens.

The sky had forgotten its own color. Its own divinity. Its own purpose.

Beneath ruined celestial laws... a lone existence opened his eyes.

Asura Cheng inhaled. Pain. Silence. Adaptation.

Something impossible moved inside his existence.

The laws around him distorted. Not cultivation. Not destiny. Not qi. Change itself.

The Forgotten God had begun awakening.
