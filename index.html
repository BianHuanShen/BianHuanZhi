CHAPTER 1

The Forgotten Heaven's Ashes

Asura Cheng awakens beneath a broken sky.

Cold ash descended from the ruined heavens.

The sky no longer possessed color. It had forgotten how to be alive.

Beneath collapsing celestial remains... a lone figure opened his eyes.

Asura Cheng inhaled. Pain. Silence. Adaptation. Something impossible moved within his existence.

The laws surrounding him distorted. Not cultivation. Not qi. Not destiny. Change itself.

The Forgotten God had begun to awaken.
